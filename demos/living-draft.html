Geology vs. Embers

Geology is the classic deep diff: heat never fades, so depth of colour means total revisions that ever touched a passage — strata of editorial sediment.

Embers lets each region cool as snapshots pass without touching it (heat × e−age/τ, τ ≈ 6 snapshots). What stays bright is where the writer’s mind is circling right now — a picture of current attention, not accumulated history. Toggle between them and watch old worries fade while fresh ones glow.

How snapshots happen

A revision commits automatically when the writer pauses for 2 seconds after a change, or every 40 keystrokes, whichever comes first. Each snapshot is chained to the last with a character diff; uncommitted typing shows as a whisper-light provisional tint until it lands.

Self-contained demo — diff engine (google/diff-match-patch) and deep-diff marker algebra inlined; no network required.
