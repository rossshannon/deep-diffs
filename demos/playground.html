Deep Diffs

The history of a document

A conventional diff compares two versions; a deep diff chains diffs across an entire revision history. Each change becomes a marker that shifts, expands and contracts as later edits land — where edits pile onto earlier edits, the highlights deepen into a heatmap of editorial attention, revealing the finessing that is usually lost between drafts. From the AVI 2010 paper by Ross Shannon, Aaron Quigley & Paddy Nixon, Deep Diffs: Visually Exploring the History of a Document.
