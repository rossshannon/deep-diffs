Deep Diffs × ProseMirror

The FUTURE-DIRECTIONS “editor decorations” direction made concrete: a real ProseMirror plugin whose state is a live list of deep-diff markers, mapped through every transaction — the editor’s own position mapping playing the role of the library’s marker transform — and rendered as stacked inline decorations that glow beneath the text as you write. Technique from Shannon, Quigley & Nixon, Deep Diffs (AVI 2010).

The plugin

Plugin state holds markers {from, to, revision, lastTouched} in document positions. Every transaction pushes them through tr.mapping — ProseMirror’s built-in operational transform, the very thing transformMarkers hand-rolls in the core library — then the DecorationSet is rebuilt so nesting depth stays true to the overlaps.

Overlapping markers are flattened into Decoration.inline segments that carry their stacking depth (data-depth, class dd-d3…), so a thrice-reworked phrase sits three markers deep: the same nested-intensity heatmap as the library’s <ins> output, live in a rich-text editor.

Geology vs. Embers

Geology is the classic deep diff: heat never fades, so depth of colour means total snapshots that ever touched a passage — strata of editorial sediment.

Embers lets each region cool as snapshots pass without touching it (heat × e−age/τ, τ = 6 snapshots). What stays bright is where your attention is circling right now.

How snapshots happen

A revision commits when the document has changed and you pause for 2 seconds (configurable snapshotMs), or when you press Commit snapshot (the commitSnapshot command). Inserted ranges are gathered from each transaction’s step maps — exact provenance, no diffing — and uncommitted typing shows as a whisper-light provisional tint until it lands.

Self-contained demo — ProseMirror (MIT, marijnh & contributors) and the deep-diffs plugin from integrations/prosemirror/ are bundled inline; no network required. Rebuild with node integrations/prosemirror/build.mjs.

On Second Thoughts

Every draft keeps a diary it never shows you. The sentences that arrived whole and were never questioned sit beside the ones that were torn down and rebuilt five times, and on the page they look exactly alike.

This editor disagrees. Beneath the text, a deep diff accumulates: pause for a moment and your edits commit as a quiet snapshot; rework the same phrase again and the highlight deepens, stratum on stratum. Where the colour pools is where your attention has been living.

Try it — rewrite the opening line, fuss over a word in the middle paragraph, then fuss over it again. The heat will find your habits.
